DiaRoutine — Diabetes Daily Management
An all-in-one daily planner combining glucose logs, prescribed medication schedules, activity, appointments, and health habits.

Target users: People who find it difficult to maintain a consistent diabetes-management routine.

Core features
- Medication reminders based on the user's prescribed schedule.
- Blood sugar logging and daily checklists.
- Water intake, walking, and activity tracking.
- Appointment and prescription-refill reminders.
- Weekly adherence summaries.
- A caregiver mode for users who choose to share their progress.
Standout feature: A personalized daily timeline showing the user's planned activities, completed tasks, and outstanding reminders in one screen.

Monetization: Premium reminders, caregiver accounts, additional profiles, and cloud synchronization.

Why it could work: It addresses the everyday organizational burden of managing a chronic condition rather than competing solely on glucose charts.
