Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026 · Applies to DiaRoutine for Android, version 1.0 and later

DiaRoutine is a daily planner for people managing diabetes, published by Hudutech Solutions (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Your information stays on your phone. We never receive, see, sell or share it.
  • No account. You never sign up or sign in.
  • No internet access. The app does not have Android’s permission to connect to the internet.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • You decide what is shared. Information leaves the app only when you tap Share and choose where to send it.

1. Information you enter

To build your daily routine, you can enter the following. All of it is optional and you can change or delete it in the app.

WhatExamples
Profile and settingsYour first name, glucose units and target range, daily water, step and activity goals, planned glucose-check and walk times, reminder preferences
MedicationsNames, strengths, forms, doses, schedule times, instructions, supply on hand, and when you marked a dose taken or skipped
Glucose readingsValues, times, meal context and any notes you add
Daily habitsGlasses of water, steps and active minutes you record
AppointmentsTitles, who they are with, dates, times, notes and reminder choices
Caregiver detailsA caregiver’s name and phone number or email, if you turn on caregiver mode

Some of this is health information. It is used only inside the app, to show your timeline, reminders and summaries.

2. Where it is stored

Everything you enter is saved in DiaRoutine’s private storage on your phone. Android keeps this storage separate from other apps, so they cannot read it.

DiaRoutine is excluded from Android’s cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your information is not copied to Google Drive or to another phone. This keeps it under your control, but it also means:

  • uninstalling the app or clearing its storage permanently deletes your information; and
  • moving to a new phone does not bring your information with it.

3. What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect personal information, health information, device identifiers, location, usage statistics or crash reports. The app has no servers to send them to.

When you download DiaRoutine, Google Play processes information under Google’s Privacy Policy. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply to you.

4. When information leaves the app

Information leaves DiaRoutine only when you choose:

  • Sharing a weekly summary. When you tap Share, the app prepares a plain-text summary (your first name if set, the date range, medication adherence, glucose averages and time in range, and water and step averages) and opens Android’s share sheet. You choose the app and the recipient. That app’s own privacy policy then applies. We never receive a copy.
  • Opening links. Links to this policy, the Terms of Use or our email address open in your browser or email app.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Permissions

PermissionWhy
NotificationsTo show reminders for doses, glucose checks, walks, appointments and refills
Alarms & reminders (optional)Lets reminders arrive on the minute instead of a few minutes late; you can allow it in the app’s Profile screen
Run at startupTo set your reminders again after your phone restarts or its time or time zone changes

DiaRoutine does not request internet access, location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

6. Security

Your information is protected by Android’s app sandbox. To keep it private, use a screen lock on your phone. Reminder notifications can show medication names on your lock screen; you can hide sensitive notification content in your phone’s notification settings.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Edit or delete entries at any time in the app.
  • Turn reminders off in Profile, or change notification settings in Android.
  • Delete everything from Settings → Apps → DiaRoutine → Storage → Clear storage (called “Clear data” on some phones), or by uninstalling the app.

Data protection laws such as the EU and UK GDPR, Kenya’s Data Protection Act 2019 and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights over personal data an organisation holds about you. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete — your information is entirely on your device. If you have questions, contact us and we will help.

8. Children

DiaRoutine is not directed to children under 13. Younger people managing diabetes should use it with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how DiaRoutine handles information — for example, by adding optional cloud backup — we will update this policy and its effective date before the change takes effect, and ask for your permission in the app before any of your information leaves your device.
