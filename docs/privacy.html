Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026 · Applies to DiaRoutine for Android, version 1.0 and later

DiaRoutine is a daily planner for people managing diabetes, published by Hudutech Solutions (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Your information stays on your phone. We never receive, see, sell or share it.
  • Backups only you can read. If you turn on backup (Premium), your data is encrypted on your phone before it goes to your own Google Drive. Only your recovery key can unlock it — not us, not Google.
  • No account with us. You never sign up or sign in to DiaRoutine.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • You decide what is shared. Information leaves the app only when you tap Share and choose where to send it.

1. Information you enter

To build your daily routine, you can enter the following. All of it is optional and you can change or delete it in the app.

WhatExamples
Profile and settingsYour first name, glucose units and target range, daily water, step and activity goals, planned glucose-check and walk times, reminder and app lock preferences
MedicationsNames, strengths, forms, doses, schedule times, instructions, supply on hand, and when you marked a dose taken or skipped
Glucose readingsValues, times, meal context and any notes you add
Daily habitsGlasses of water, steps and active minutes you record
AppointmentsTitles, who they are with, dates, times, notes and reminder choices
Caregiver detailsA caregiver’s name and phone number or email, if you turn on caregiver mode

Some of this is health information. It is used only inside the app, to show your timeline, reminders and summaries.

2. Where it is stored

Everything you enter is saved in DiaRoutine’s private storage on your phone. Android keeps this storage separate from other apps, so they cannot read it.

DiaRoutine is excluded from Android’s own cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Unless you turn on DiaRoutine’s encrypted backup (section 4), uninstalling the app or clearing its storage permanently deletes your information, and moving to a new phone does not bring it with you.

3. What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect personal information, health information, device identifiers, location, usage statistics or crash reports. DiaRoutine has no servers of ours to send them to. The only times the app connects to the internet are for the optional encrypted backup (section 4) and for Google Play purchases (section 5).

When you download DiaRoutine, Google Play processes information under Google’s Privacy Policy. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply to you.

4. Encrypted backup to Google Drive

Backup is optional and part of DiaRoutine Premium. When you turn it on:

  • Your data is encrypted on your phone before it leaves, with AES-256 encryption and a key derived from a recovery key that DiaRoutine creates for you and shows you once. The recovery key is kept on your phone protected by the Android Keystore, so weekly backups can run on their own. It is never sent to us or to Google.
  • The encrypted file is stored in your own Google Drive, in a hidden folder that only DiaRoutine can use. The app asks Google only for access to that folder — it cannot see or change any of your other Drive files. Google stores the file under Google’s Privacy Policy, but cannot read it.
  • It runs weekly on Wi-Fi or mobile data, and whenever you tap “Back up now”. Each backup replaces the previous one. The app shows your Google account’s email address so you can see where backups go; it reads this from Google and keeps it only on your phone.
  • Only your recovery key can restore it. If you lose the key, the backup cannot be opened by anyone, including us — we cannot reset or recover it.

You can turn backup off, show your recovery key, or delete the backup from Drive at any time in Profile → Backup & restore. You can also remove it from Google Drive’s settings under Manage apps → DiaRoutine → Delete hidden app data.

5. Premium and purchases

DiaRoutine Premium is a subscription sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment, the free trial and cancellations under its own terms and privacy policy. On your phone, the app receives only Google Play’s confirmation that you have an active subscription, and remembers it so Premium works offline. We never receive your payment details.

6. When information leaves the app

Information leaves DiaRoutine only when you choose:

  • Sharing a weekly summary. When you tap Share, the app prepares a plain-text summary (your first name if set, the date range, medication adherence, glucose averages and time in range, and water and step averages) and opens Android’s share sheet. You choose the app and the recipient. That app’s own privacy policy then applies. We never receive a copy.
  • Opening links. Links to this policy, the Terms of Use or our email address open in your browser or email app.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

7. Permissions

InternetOnly to back up to and restore from your Google Drive, if you turn backup on, and for Google Play purchases
NotificationsTo show reminders for doses, glucose checks, walks, appointments and refills
Alarms & reminders (optional)Lets reminders arrive on the minute instead of a few minutes late; you can allow it in the app’s Profile screen

DiaRoutine does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

8. Security

Your information is protected by Android’s app sandbox. To keep it private, use a screen lock on your phone, and consider turning on DiaRoutine’s app lock in Profile, which asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock each time the app is opened and hides its preview in your recent apps. Reminder notifications can show medication names on your lock screen; you can hide sensitive notification content in your phone’s notification settings.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Edit or delete entries at any time in the app.
  • Turn reminders off in Profile, or change notification settings in Android.
  • Delete everything on your phone from Settings → Apps → DiaRoutine → Storage → Clear storage (called “Clear data” on some phones), or by uninstalling the app.
  • Delete your backup from Profile → Backup & restore, or from Google Drive’s settings.

Data protection laws such as the EU and UK GDPR, Kenya’s Data Protection Act 2019 and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights over personal data an organisation holds about you. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete — your information is on your device and, if you use backup, encrypted in your own Google Drive where you control it. If you have questions, contact us and we will help.

10. Children

DiaRoutine is not directed to children under 13. Younger people managing diabetes should use it with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how DiaRoutine handles information, we will update this policy and its effective date before the change takes effect, and ask for your permission in the app before any new kind of information leaves your device.
