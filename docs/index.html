Everything your routine needs

Built for the daily work of living with diabetes, not just the numbers.

One daily timeline

Doses, glucose checks, your walk and appointments in time order, each marked done, due, overdue or missed.

Medication reminders

Set each medication as prescribed. Mark doses taken from the notification, and get warned before you run out.

Glucose log

Log readings in mg/dL or mmol/L with meal context, see them against your target, and follow your 7-day trend.

Appointments & refills

Check-ups, lab tests and eye exams with reminders ahead of time, plus refill alerts based on your supply.

Weekly summary

See how consistently you took your medication, your streaks, time in range and habit trends.

Caregiver mode

Send a plain weekly summary to someone who supports you, by any app you choose. Nothing is sent automatically.

A closer look

Calm, legible screens in light and dark, with large touch targets and screen-reader labels throughout.

Private by design

Your health information is yours. DiaRoutine keeps it on your phone and never sends it anywhere you didn’t choose.

Read the privacy policy

  • No account or sign-up
  • No internet access in the app
  • No ads, analytics or tracking
  • Optional app lock with your fingerprint or face
  • You choose what to share, and with whom

DiaRoutine is an organizer for the routine your healthcare team gives you. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Always follow your healthcare professional’s instructions.
