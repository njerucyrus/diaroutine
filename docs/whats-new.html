Release notes for DiaRoutine on Android

1.0.0 — first release

DiaRoutine brings the everyday work of managing diabetes — doses, glucose checks, water, walks, appointments and refills — into one calm daily timeline, so you can see what’s done, what’s due and what’s next.

Your day in one timeline

  • Medication doses, glucose checks, your daily walk and appointments, in time order
  • Each item marked done, due now, overdue or missed — catch up with one tap
  • A week strip to look back at past days and plan ahead

Medication reminders

  • Add each medication as prescribed: name, strength, form, dose and times
  • A reminder when each dose is due, with “Mark as taken” right in the notification
  • Log as-needed medication whenever you take it
  • Track your supply and get a reminder before you run out

Glucose log

  • Log readings in mg/dL or mmol/L, with meal context and notes
  • See every reading against the target range your care team set
  • Planned daily checks with reminders, and a 7-day trend, average and time in range

Water, steps, activity and appointments

  • Tap to add a glass of water; record steps and active minutes against your daily goals
  • Check-ups, lab tests and eye or foot exams in one place, with reminders a day or a few hours before

Weekly summary and caregiver mode

  • Doses taken day by day, current and longest streaks, glucose time in range, lows and highs, and habit trends
  • Send a plain-text summary to a family member or carer through any app you choose — nothing is ever sent automatically

Private and secure

  • No account, no ads, no tracking — your health information stays on your phone
  • Optional app lock with your fingerprint, face or screen lock; the app’s preview is hidden in recent apps
  • Your fingerprint and face are checked by your phone; DiaRoutine never sees them

Getting started

  • A short welcome, an optional example routine to explore, and a guided tour of the Today screen — replay it any time from Profile
  • Light and dark themes, large touch targets and screen-reader labels throughout, on phones and tablets

DiaRoutine Premium — free for 3 months

  • Encrypted Google Drive backup, weekly and on demand, protected by a recovery key only you hold. Restoring is always free.
  • Missed-dose follow-ups, and a 10-minute snooze on every dose reminder
  • 30- and 90-day insights for adherence, glucose and habits

Eligible new subscribers get 3 months free; the subscription then renews monthly or yearly until you cancel in Google Play. See the terms.

Good to know

  • Requires Android 8.0 or later.
  • For reminders on the minute, allow On-time alerts in Profile → Reminders.
  • Restoring a backup needs the same Google account and your recovery key.
  • DiaRoutine is an organizer for the routine your healthcare team gives you. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.
